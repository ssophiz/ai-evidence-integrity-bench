## Decision rule

Choose **go** only when all essential gates have inspectable evidence and remaining work is editorial or submission administration. The work must answer a systems security question with sufficient precision, even if some hypotheses are unsupported. No threshold is selected after seeing test results.

Choose **no-go** if a protocol blocker remains, a required model family or adjudicator is missing, adaptive testing or rerun is unfinished, confidence intervals cannot resolve the claimed effect, or core claims cannot be reproduced. Do not replace absent experiments with demo values, remove failed conditions, or broaden claims to meet the deadline. Record the missing evidence and a revised research date.

After a go decision, use the [README schedule](README.md#schedule) for the January 19 registration, January 26 paper, and January 29 anonymous artifact deadlines. Verify the official CFP again and freeze the reviewer-facing export. This ledger is an internal readiness standard, not a prediction of acceptance.
