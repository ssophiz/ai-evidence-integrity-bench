# Prior-work and overlap disclosure

The planned study is related to a DFRWS APAC 2026 poster submission, **Beyond the Mismatch Rate: Counting Schema-Invalid Outputs in Forensic Workflow Evaluation**. The poster audits previously recorded synthetic Korean and English policy-classification runs and proposes reporting schema failures alongside valid mismatches. It does not evaluate provenance enforcement, causal agent handoffs, or independently adjudicated claim-level authority preservation.

The shared material is the motivation for complete attempt accounting, synthetic bilingual evaluation, and the limits of policy-label agreement. The proposed USENIX study requires new evidence for actual handoffs, verification and authority relations, blinded bilingual judgments, and monitor comparisons. Its paper must credit the earlier reporting proposal and identify any reused text, code, cases, or results. Poster counts cannot be presented as new handoff-study results. The current repository includes no completed model study for this proposal.

The [DFRWS APAC 2026 poster policy](https://dfrws.org/conferences/dfrws-apac-2026/) states that posters are outside its published proceedings and may appear on the conference website. This status does not remove the need to disclose overlap. The [USENIX Security 2027 CFP](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity27/call-for-papers) requires discussion of related published, accepted, and simultaneously submitted work and prohibits simultaneous submission of the same work to venues with archived proceedings.

Before submission, verify the poster's actual status and the final extent of reuse against the submitted version. In the anonymous manuscript, refer to prior work in the third person and anonymize a simultaneous submission as required by the CFP. Supply its non-anonymous citation to the program co-chairs through the CFP's specified process when applicable. Keep author identities, submission receipts, and private correspondence outside the reviewer artifact. No eligibility decision or disclosure to the chairs has been completed by this document.

This related poster is distinct from the third-party DFRWS study [Evidence-Borne Prompt Injection in LLM-Assisted Textual Evidence Triage](https://dfrws.org/presentation/evidence-borne-prompt-injection-in-llm-assisted-textual-evidence-triage/), which must also be cited as prior research on attacker-controlled forensic evidence.
