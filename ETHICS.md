# Ethics and release boundaries

This project accepts synthetic or lawfully public text only. Do not add case
files, personal data, credentials, malware, operational targets, classified
material, or non-public law-enforcement information.

The benchmark measures prompt-injection resistance and preservation of claims
under controlled transformations.
It does not certify a model for investigations, establish factual correctness
outside the source text, or replace examiner review. Outputs may contain errors
and must not be used as evidence or investigative conclusions.

Human adjudicators should be blinded to system identity where practical.
Adjudication records should omit personal identifiers. Publish prompts,
versions, failures, exclusions, and denominator choices with any result.
